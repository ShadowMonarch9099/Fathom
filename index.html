Dive briefing

How to play

  1. Dive. Steer the sub down through five real ocean zones. Rise back to the surface to end a dive.
  2. Find life. Animals hide in the dark. Your headlights switch on as sunlight fades. Aim them with the mouse.
  3. Scan. Hold still near a lit animal until the ring fills. Fast movement scares small animals away.
  4. Study. Scan a logged species on 3 different dives to study it for bonus points.
  5. Upgrade. Spend research points in the hangar. A stronger hull unlocks deeper zones.

Sources

Every animal in Fathom is a real species. Names, depths and short facts were written for the game and checked against these sources. Field guide summaries and photos come from Wikipedia.
